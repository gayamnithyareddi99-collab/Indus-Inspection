Indus-Inspection was not designed in a boardroom. It was engineered from real manufacturing environments where inspection bottlenecks, audit pressure, and supplier deviations directly impact production efficiency.

Founder Shamnas Hussain brings hands-on UK automotive manufacturing experience combined with advanced engineering management expertise. Having worked within structured quality systems, Lean manufacturing, and CMM-based inspection environments, he identified a persistent gap:

Indus-Inspection directly solves this challenge by embedding ISO and IATF discipline into structured QR-driven workflows, enabling compliance-by-design instead of documentation-after-the-fact.

PROCESS EXECUTION FRAMEWORK

How The Indus Inspection Platform Works

A disciplined digital inspection workflow that converts shop-floor activity into structured compliance intelligence.

01

Scan & Load Template

Operator scans workstation QR code. The system automatically loads the correct inspection template, tolerance rules, and revision-controlled documentation.

02

Execute Digital Inspection

Inspection data is captured digitally with mandatory validation rules. Incorrect values cannot be approved beyond defined tolerance thresholds.

03

Real-Time Validation

Built-in SPC logic detects deviations instantly. Supervisors receive alerts when trends indicate early-stage process drift.

04

Audit-Grade Output

Approved records are structured and stored as compliance-ready documentation aligned with ISO / IATF audit expectations.

DIGITAL QUALITY EXECUTION LAYER

Operational Inspection Intelligence
Engineered for Industrial Control

A structured execution layer between shop-floor activity and audit-grade compliance architecture — replacing manual tracking, spreadsheet dependency, and fragmented documentation systems.

01

QR Workflow Routing

Each workstation or inspection point is mapped to a unique QR identity. Scanning automatically loads the correct inspection template, tolerance rules, and revision-controlled documentation.

Eliminates manual template selection errors and ensures disciplined execution at the point of activity.

Automation
02

Clause Mapping Engine

Inspection records are digitally mapped to ISO 9001 and IATF 16949 clauses, creating built-in audit traceability.

During external audits, structured digital evidence replaces manual file compilation, reducing preparation time significantly.

Compliance
03

SPC Intelligence

Real-time tolerance validation prevents incorrect approvals before submission. Statistical trend monitoring detects early-stage process drift.

Automated alerts notify supervisors when deviation thresholds are exceeded — enabling preventive control.

Analytics
04

Supplier Module

Manage inbound material inspections, supplier scorecards, and structured First Article Inspection (FAI) outputs.

Performance dashboards improve supplier accountability and reduce recurring quality deviations.

Enables data-driven quality improvement rather than reactive correction.
